04 / MEANINGFUL OUTPUTS

Measure, derive, act

Measured

Air temperature, RH, pH, EC, flow, pilot solution temperature and PPFD.

Derived

VPD from temperature/RH; DLI from integrated PPFD. TDS is generally converted from EC.

Action

Local pump stop on confirmed level/leak/flow fault; alarm and human review when probes disagree.
